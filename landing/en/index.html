Frequently asked questions

What people ask most

Is Netio affiliated with Mercado Libre?

No. Netio is an independent tool and has no relationship with Mercado Libre. It does not use a Mercado Libre API: the rates come from public seller guides, so the figures are estimates.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser. Your costs and products never leave your computer and Netio has no servers of its own. The only thing that goes out is the PRO payment, handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, and only if you choose to pay.

Do I need an RFC to use Netio?

No. Netio does not ask for an RFC or any kind of account. If you have an RFC registered with Mercado Libre, Netio also shows the 2026 withholdings (8% IVA and 2.5% ISR). Confirm with your accountant how they apply to you.

Which browsers does it work in?

In desktop Chrome (Windows, Mac and Linux), including Chromebook. It should also work in desktop Chromium-based browsers such as Edge or Brave, though we mostly test on Chrome. It does not work on phones, or in Firefox or Safari.

How does the PRO trial work?

When you install Netio you get full PRO for 7 days. It asks for no email or card, and the trial runs in your own browser. When it ends, you keep the free plan and your saved costs.

How much is PRO and how do I cancel?

PRO costs $89 MXN per month or $699 MXN per year. To cancel, open Netio’s options and tap “Manage subscription / pay”. You can cancel there at any time. More details on the Support page.

Are the numbers exact?

They are estimates. The exact percentage depends on your category and account, and the date of the rates appears on every card. Use them to decide with better information, not as an accounting figure.
